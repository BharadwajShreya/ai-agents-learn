# TechNova Inc. — Product Documentation

## Product: NovaMail — AI Email Assistant

### Overview
NovaMail is TechNova's flagship AI-powered email assistant. It uses a fine-tuned LLM to help professionals draft, summarize, and prioritize emails. NovaMail integrates with Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail.

### Key Features

#### 1. Smart Compose
NovaMail can draft complete email replies based on:
- The incoming email content
- Your writing style (learned from your sent emails over 2 weeks)
- Calendar context (e.g., "I'm free Thursday afternoon" from your actual calendar)

**Accuracy**: 87% of drafts are sent with zero edits (internal benchmark, Q1 2026)

#### 2. Email Summarization
For long email threads (5+ messages), NovaMail generates:
- A 2-sentence summary
- Action items extracted from the thread
- Decision points that need your input

**Supported Languages**: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean

#### 3. Priority Inbox
NovaMail scores each email on a 1-10 urgency scale based on:
- Sender relationship (boss = high, newsletter = low)
- Content keywords ("urgent", "deadline", "ASAP")
- Historical response patterns (emails you always reply to quickly)

### Technical Architecture
- **Model**: Fine-tuned Llama 3.2 8B (quantized to 4-bit for edge deployment)
- **Latency**: < 200ms for draft generation (P95)
- **Privacy**: All processing happens on-device for free tier. Cloud processing available for enterprise features.
- **API Rate Limits**: 
  - Free tier: 50 drafts/day
  - Pro ($9.99/month): 500 drafts/day
  - Enterprise (custom): Unlimited

### Known Limitations
- Does not handle attachments (planned for v2.1)
- Calendar integration requires OAuth re-auth every 90 days
- Smart Compose quality degrades for emails longer than 2000 words
- Not recommended for legal or compliance-sensitive communications

### Roadmap (2026)

- Q3: Slack integration
- Q4: Multi-language Smart Compose (currently English only for drafting)
