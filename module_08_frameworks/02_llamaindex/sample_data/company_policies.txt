# TechNova Inc. — Company Policies Handbook (Excerpt)

## Remote Work Policy

TechNova supports a hybrid work model. All employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. 
Full remote status requires VP-level approval and is limited to roles classified as "location-independent."

### Eligibility
- Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period
- Contractors on engagements longer than 6 months (with manager approval)
- Interns are NOT eligible for remote work

### Equipment
The company provides a $1,500 one-time stipend for home office setup. This covers:
- Monitor, keyboard, mouse
- Ergonomic chair (up to $500)
- Internet upgrade costs (up to $50/month reimbursement)

### Communication Standards
- All remote employees must be available on Slack during core hours (10am-4pm local time)
- Video must be ON for all client-facing meetings
- Daily async standup posts are required in the team's #standup channel

## PTO (Paid Time Off) Policy

TechNova offers unlimited PTO with the following guidelines:
- Minimum 15 days off per year (enforced by HR)
- Maximum consecutive days: 10 business days without VP approval
- Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for periods longer than 3 days
- Blackout periods: Last 2 weeks of each quarter (Q1-Q4 close)

### Sick Leave
- Unlimited sick days (no documentation needed for 1-2 days)
- 3+ consecutive sick days require a doctor's note
- Mental health days count as sick leave — no questions asked

## Performance Review Process

Reviews happen twice per year:
- **Mid-Year Review** (June): Informal check-in, goal adjustment
- **Annual Review** (December): Formal evaluation, compensation discussion

### Rating Scale
1. **Needs Improvement** — Performance consistently below expectations
2. **Meets Expectations** — Solid, reliable contributor
3. **Exceeds Expectations** — Goes above and beyond regularly  
4. **Exceptional** — Top 10% performer, promotion candidate

### Promotion Criteria
- Must be rated "Exceeds Expectations" or above for 2 consecutive review cycles
- Must have sponsorship from a senior leader outside their direct reporting chain
- Must complete the company's leadership development program (for management track)
